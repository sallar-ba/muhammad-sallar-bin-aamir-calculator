# Jobs To Be Done

Each job describes what the [Personal Loan Shopper](app-roles.md) needs to get done, whether or not this app exists. "Served by" lists the [user stories](user-stories.md) that address each job.

## J-1 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When I have loan offers from several lenders, each with a different interest rate, length and fee, I want to know which one will cost me the least in total, so I can accept the cheapest offer instead of the one that only looks cheapest."

Why it is real: lenders quote the rate, length and fee separately, and the lowest rate is not always the cheapest loan once the fee is counted.

Served by: S-4, S-7, S-12 (MVP); S-9, S-11 (later)

## J-2 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When a lender takes a fee out of the loan before paying it to me, I want to know exactly how much cash I will actually receive, so I can be sure it covers the expense I am borrowing for."

Why it is real: personal loan processing fees are commonly deducted before payout, so the cash that arrives is less than the amount borrowed.

Served by: S-3

## J-3 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When I am deciding whether to take on a loan, I want to know exactly what I will have to pay every month, so I can check that it fits my budget before I commit."

Why it is real: a missed payment brings penalties and damages credit history, so whether the payment is affordable matters as much as the price.

Served by: S-1

## J-4 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When one offer has a lower monthly payment because it runs for longer, I want to know how much more it will cost me overall, so I can decide whether the smaller payment is worth the extra cost."

Why it is real: stretching a loan lowers each payment but adds interest, and the extra is easy to miss when only the monthly figure is quoted.

Served by: S-5

## J-5 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When a lender gives me their own figures for a loan, I want to check them independently, so I can sign knowing they are right and that no cost has been left out."

Why it is real: the lender is the one selling the loan, and its headline figures often leave the fee out.

Served by: S-2, S-6 (MVP); S-10 (later)

## J-6 (Personal Loan Shopper)

"When I am working out what a loan would cost me, I want to keep my financial details to myself, so I can avoid being chased by lenders and brokers I have not chosen."

Why it is real: many loan comparison sites collect contact details and pass them on to lenders, which leads to unwanted calls and messages.

Served by: S-8
