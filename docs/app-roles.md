# App Roles

## Personal Loan Shopper

A Personal Loan Shopper is an adult who needs to borrow a fixed sum for a planned expense, such as medical bills, a wedding, home repairs or paying off credit card debt, and who has received two or three personal loan offers from different lenders, each quoted with a different interest rate, loan length and upfront fee. They are not a finance expert, and they often check the numbers on their phone shortly before they have to accept or decline an offer. They can enter the amount borrowed, annual interest rate, loan length and upfront fee for one offer, add up to two more offers to compare, and remove them again. They can see, for each offer, the monthly payment (EMI), the amount they would actually receive after the fee, the total interest, the total repaid and the total cost of borrowing, and how the total repaid splits into the amount received, the upfront fee and the interest. When their offers are for the same amount borrowed, they can see which offer has the lowest total cost and which has the lowest monthly payment, and how much more each of the other offers costs. They must never see a result calculated from invalid or incomplete input, see a total cost that leaves out the upfront fee they entered, or have the loan details they enter sent anywhere off their device.

## Why there is one role

The app answers one question: which of these new loan offers should I take? Only the Personal Loan Shopper asks it. These people were considered and left out:

| Considered | Their situation | Why they are not a role in this app |
|---|---|---|
| Existing borrower | Already repaying a loan and deciding whether to pay it off early or move it to another lender | Needs the outstanding balance, prepayment penalties and remaining-term maths. That is a different question from choosing between new offers. |
| Loan agent or advisor | Shows loan options to many clients | Needs client records, branding and sharing, which is a different product. Comparing offers for one person is already covered by the Personal Loan Shopper. |
| Lender | Sets the rates and fees | Has its own pricing tools and is not choosing between offers. |
